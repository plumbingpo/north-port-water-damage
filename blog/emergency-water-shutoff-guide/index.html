Emergency Water Shutoff Guide in North Port, FL

Home / Blog / How to Shut Off Your Water in an Emergency in North Port, FL

Stopping the flow of water is the quickest way to limit damage during a water emergency. You could avoid extensive property damage and save thousands of dollars just by knowing where your shutoff valve is and how it works. If you own a home in North Port, FL, find your shutoff valve and practice using it now, not during a leak.

Locating Your Main Water Shutoff Valve

In most North Port homes, the main water shutoff valve is located in one of these areas: near the front of the house where the water line enters from the street, in the garage along an interior wall, in the basement or crawl space near the foundation wall, in a utility room or laundry area, or in a ground-level access box near the street (the meter shutoff). Two types are typical: gate valves, which have a round handle, and ball valves, which have a lever. Ball valves are more reliable and can be turned off with a quarter turn.

The Quick Way to Stop the Water

Several clockwise (right) turns of the handle, until it stops, will close a gate valve. With a ball valve, swing the lever until it is perpendicular to the pipe. A valve that will not move should not be forced, as breaking it would make things worse. Go to the meter and close it there with a meter key or an adjustable wrench.

Fixture and Appliance Shutoffs

A separate valve on most appliances and fixtures lets you cut water to just that spot while the rest of the house stays on. They are found near water heaters, behind washing machines and toilets, and under sinks. Knowing these locations allows you to stop localized leaks quickly while maintaining water service to the rest of your home.
